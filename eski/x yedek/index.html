FAQ

Questions before you create your first Mailmyra signature?

Here are the details most teams want to clarify before they start.

Is Mailmyra ready to use without a backend?

The generator runs as a frontend demo. Users can preview details freely, but exporting signature HTML requires login and an active yearly plan.

Can I use my own logo and profile image?

Yes. Add hosted image URLs for profile photos, company logos and handwritten signature visuals in the Images step.

Do templates change the actual signature layout?

Yes. Template selection updates the preview layout with class-based variations while keeping your entered data intact.

Can the signature match Mailmyra brand colors?

The style controls support custom theme, text and link colors. The default palette follows Mailmyra blue, warm sand and neutral gray.
